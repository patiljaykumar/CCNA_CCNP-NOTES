- ### WAN technologies define L1 & L2 protocols used to comm over long distances
    
    - ### **Leased-line WANs** (rarely used today)
        
        - L1 service offers dedicated, private. delivers bits in both directions(uses 2 pairs of wires), at predetermined speed, using full-duplex logic (act as crossover Ethernet link). 
            
        - not sure- can be P2P leased line btw 2 sites/ Internet Leased Line/ MPLS & SDWAN
        - eg- telco leases use of leased line/circuit/link to customer, but customer does not permanently own line
            
        - alternate names- Leased circuit, Serial link(for P2P- serial int), P2P link(2 nodes only), T1(type of LL with 1.544Mbps), WAN link, Private line(data cannot copy by other telco customers)

        - <ins>Adv</ins>\- simple, high quality, private | <ins>DisAdv</ins>\- slow, high cost, more time for install
            
    - ### **Ethernet WANs**
        
        - uses shared Ethernet backbone. (fiber)Ethernet link leaves customer building & connects to some nearby SP(Service Provider) location(mostly its SW) called PoP(Point of Presence)
            
            - <img src="../../_resources/23f44df3e870fa37583bf09384c8ec33.png" alt="23f44df3e870fa37583bf09384c8ec33.png" width="343" height="118" class="jop-noMdConv">
        - WAN service(like Ethernet crossover cable) logically behaves like P2P connection btw 2 routers, physically fiber Ethernet link
            
        - **E-Line(Ethernet-Line)**\- term for P2P Ethernet WAN
            
        - **EoMPLS(Ethernet over Multiprotocol Label Switching)**\-
